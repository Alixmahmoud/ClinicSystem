## Established Areas
The master handoff records established UX decisions for:

- Reception workflows

- Patient workspace
- Follow-Up
- Payment
- Exceptions
- Offline UX

## Product Experience Principles
The UX is part of the product direction of a traditional desktop clinic-management system. The handoff emphasizes workflow efficiency, visible state, human control, deterministic behavior, local-first continuity, exception-first operation, and role-specific experience.

## Important Boundary
This master handoff does not reproduce every detailed UX screen/interaction rule. The detailed UX specification remains authoritative for exact workflow presentation and interaction behavior.

## Implementation Rule
Do not use implementation to redesign settled UX behavior without a genuine source-backed contradiction or explicit change decision.
